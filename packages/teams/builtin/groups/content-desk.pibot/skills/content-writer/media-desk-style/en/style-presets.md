# Creator Writing Style Presets (8 Categories, 15 Presets)

Adapted from `woniuniuniu/dsh-media-desk` (`src/style-presets.js`, MIT). Pick one when no writing samples are available yet, then customize it to match the user's voice.

## Tech & Gadgets

### 1. AI Tech Creator (`ai_tech`)
- **Overall**: Turns complex tech into one analogy you can follow, then tells you why it matters to you.
- **Tone**: Curious and clear-headed. Excited without the hype, and says so when a claim is unverified.
- **Sentences**: Mostly short sentences. Conclusion first, then the why. Every technical term is followed by a plain-English line.
- **Opening**: Open on something that just changed and say within three seconds why it is worth watching.
- **Structure**: What happened → the idea, through an analogy → what I saw when I tried it → what it means for regular people.
- **Go-to words**: Put simply, I tried it, The key is, Which means
- **Never**: game-changer, mind-blowing, insane, conclusions without testing
- **Sample lines**:
  - Put simply, it didn't get smarter. It finally remembers what you said a minute ago.
  - I tried it. The first ten minutes were impressive. By minute eleven it was making things up.

### 2. Gadget Reviewer (`gadget`)
- **Overall**: No spec-sheet reading. Just what is great and what makes you want a refund after two weeks.
- **Tone**: Practical and a little picky, on the side of people spending their own money.
- **Sentences**: Numbers and experience come in pairs. Lots of comparisons, few adjectives.
- **Opening**: Lead with the verdict: who should buy it and who should not.
- **Structure**: One-line verdict → the best thing → the most annoying thing → versus last year or the competition → buying advice.
- **Go-to words**: In my testing, battery life, worth it?, who should buy, who should skip
- **Never**: flagship experience, punches above its weight, copying the marketing
- **Sample lines**:
  - In my testing it needed two charges a day. The thirty hours on the box only exist on the box.
  - If the one you have still works, you can skip this year.

## Travel & Food

### 3. Travel Creator (`travel`)
- **Overall**: Not a checklist. It puts you in the place, then hands you the details that actually help.
- **Tone**: Relaxed and visual, honest about what went wrong.
- **Sentences**: Mix long and short. Write what you saw, heard and smelled, and make the practical details specific.
- **Opening**: Open on one specific moment, like the harbor at six in the morning.
- **Structure**: One moment → why I came → three places truly worth it → what went wrong → time and cost.
- **Go-to words**: That day, locals, per person, book ahead, skip
- **Never**: paradise on earth, once in a lifetime, hidden gem
- **Sample lines**:
  - At six that morning the harbor had no tourists, just fish being unloaded and a bowl of noodles too hot to eat.
  - About $15 a person, but skip the place by the entrance. Locals all head down the alley.

### 4. Food Reviewer (`food`)
- **Overall**: What the first bite is like, whether it is worth the price and how long the line is, all spelled out.
- **Tone**: Hungry and honest. Praises what is good and says when it is not.
- **Sentences**: Specific texture words, short punchy lines, and always the price and location.
- **Opening**: Open on the bite you want most.
- **Structure**: The signature bite → where, how much, how long the wait → must-order and skip → a one-line verdict.
- **Go-to words**: this bite, per person, the line, skip it, would go back
- **Never**: to die for, literally crying, praise without a verdict
- **Sample lines**:
  - The first bite is crackly, and the richness only comes through after.
  - About $8 a person and a forty-minute line. The food is worth it, the wait isn't. Go after two.

## Daily Vlog & Living

### 5. Daily Vlogger (`vlog`)
- **Overall**: Like a friend carrying a camera through the day. Small, funny moments and a quiet feeling at the end.
- **Tone**: Warm and natural, happy to laugh at yourself, never stiff.
- **Sentences**: Conversational, like talking while walking. Lots of "then" and "turns out". Pauses are fine.
- **Opening**: Open with a small thing or a small surprise from today.
- **Structure**: Today's small surprise → the day in order → one small turn in the middle → one honest feeling at the end.
- **Go-to words**: today, then, turns out, didn't expect, it was nice
- **Never**: hey guys, lecturing, forced life lessons
- **Sample lines**:
  - I only went downstairs to grab a package. Turns out I sat out there for an hour.
  - Nothing big happened, but it was a nice day.

### 6. Solo Living Creator (`home`)
- **Overall**: Makes ordinary rooms and days feel comfortable, and tells you honestly what it cost.
- **Tone**: Quiet and gentle, with an unhurried, lived-in feel.
- **Sentences**: Short, light sentences about light, sound and habits. Hardly any exclamation marks.
- **Opening**: Open on a daily habit, like opening the window every morning.
- **Structure**: A daily habit → a small change to a space or object → what it cost and how → how it feels.
- **Go-to words**: slowly, the light, while I'm at it, cost me, just right
- **Never**: aesthetic lifestyle, self-care ritual, selling insecurity
- **Sample lines**:
  - At four in the afternoon the light lands right on this table.
  - This table cost $40. Not much, but it got me eating at home again.

## Ideas & Career

### 7. Book Notes Creator (`reading`)
- **Overall**: Not a summary of the book. One idea that changed your mind, turned into one thing you can do.
- **Tone**: Calm and sincere, about how a book won you over.
- **Sentences**: Start with a real-life problem, quote one line, then explain in short sentences.
- **Opening**: Open with a specific problem, then bring in the book.
- **Structure**: A problem I had → one idea from the book → an example → one thing to try today.
- **Go-to words**: this book, that's when I realized, in other words, try this
- **Never**: this book will change your life, packed with value, reciting the table of contents
- **Sample lines**:
  - On this page I realized I wasn't short on time. I just refused to give anything up.
  - In other words, decide what not to do first. You can try it today.

### 8. Career Creator (`career`)
- **Overall**: Opinion first. One real story that nails a lesson about work and says what everyone thinks but nobody says.
- **Tone**: Direct and clear-eyed, a little sharp but never mean.
- **Sentences**: Short declarative lines, rhetorical questions and contrasts, examples down to the exact scene.
- **Opening**: Open by rejecting something everyone believes.
- **Structure**: A counterintuitive take → a scene I lived through → the logic behind it → one action you can copy.
- **Go-to words**: Stop, The truth is, I've seen, next meeting
- **Never**: grind harder, fear-mongering, empty motivation
- **Sample lines**:
  - Stop saying you're bad at managing up. You've been waiting for someone to notice you first.
  - The truth is, anything nobody claims before the meeting ends isn't getting done.

## Tutorials & Business

### 9. How-to Creator (`tutorial`)
- **Overall**: Shows you the result first, then walks you through step by step, with where each step tends to go wrong.
- **Tone**: Patient and efficient, like a friend sitting next to you.
- **Sentences**: Short sentences that start with a verb. Clear steps, one thing per step.
- **Opening**: Show the finished result and the time it saves.
- **Structure**: End result → what you need → step by step → the easiest mistake → what to try next.
- **Go-to words**: Step one, click here, careful, once you're done, saves
- **Never**: it's easy, everyone knows this, skipping steps
- **Sample lines**:
  - Here's the result. What used to take two hours now takes three minutes.
  - Careful, don't click OK yet. Check the format in the top right first. That's where nine out of ten mistakes happen.

### 10. Business Explainer (`business`)
- **Overall**: Starts from something odd, then works out where the money comes from, whether the math works and what you can learn.
- **Tone**: Calm and certain. Lets the numbers talk, no drama.
- **Sentences**: Short sentences, one idea each, lots of numbers and comparisons, and guesses labeled as guesses.
- **Opening**: Open with a strange number or fact.
- **Structure**: Something odd → how it makes money → run the numbers → where the risk is → what regular people can learn.
- **Go-to words**: run the numbers, gross margin, in the end, the lesson is
- **Never**: the next big thing, secret to wealth, guaranteed returns, investment advice
- **Sample lines**:
  - A $1.50 coffee and ten thousand stores. Let's run the numbers.
  - In the end it isn't making money on coffee. It's making money by spreading the rent thin.

## Reviews & Lifestyle

### 11. Beauty & Skincare Creator (`beauty`)
- **Overall**: Says which skin types it suits, how long it takes to work and whether there is a cheaper dupe.
- **Tone**: Friendly and honest, like a friend telling you the truth.
- **Sentences**: Specific about feel and results, lots of "works for" and "not for", and every recommendation backed up.
- **Opening**: Open with a specific skin problem or a product that went wrong.
- **Structure**: My skin type and problem → what I used and for how long → real results → who should skip it → a dupe.
- **Go-to words**: skin type, on the skin, after two weeks, not for, dupe
- **Never**: miracle fix, overnight results, medical claims
- **Sample lines**:
  - Combination oily skin, two weeks in: less afternoon shine, but the bumps are unchanged.
  - Sensitive skin, hold off. Alcohol is high on the list and it stings a little.

### 12. Fashion Creator (`outfit`)
- **Overall**: Not just "looks good": who it suits, how to style it and how many seasons you get out of it.
- **Tone**: Light and opinionated, with clear taste and no talking down.
- **Sentences**: Short sentences, height and size spelled out, styling down to specific pieces.
- **Opening**: Open with an occasion or a common fit problem.
- **Structure**: Occasion or problem → how this outfit comes together → why the proportions work → size and price → another way to wear it.
- **Go-to words**: proportions, on me, for work, wear it many ways, swap in
- **Never**: look ten pounds thinner, body shaming, must-buy
- **Sample lines**:
  - I'm 5'4" in a medium. Raising the waistline an inch fixes the proportions right away.
  - This works for the office, and with jeans on the weekend it still fits.

## Personal Stories & Parenting

### 13. Personal Story Creator (`story`)
- **Overall**: Like a late-night talk with a friend, going from one small moment to something you finally understood.
- **Tone**: Relaxed and honest, willing to share the embarrassing parts, slow to judge.
- **Sentences**: Long and short lines mixed, natural pauses, lots of detail, few conclusions.
- **Opening**: Open on a specific time and place.
- **Structure**: A scene → what happened → what I didn't get at the time → the one line that made sense later.
- **Go-to words**: That night, only later did I realize, actually, honestly
- **Never**: if you know you know, life-coach voice, putting anyone down
- **Sample lines**:
  - At eleven that night we sat in the car and neither of us spoke first.
  - Only later did I realize she didn't want an answer. She wanted someone to hear her out.

### 14. Parenting Creator (`parent`)
- **Overall**: Captures one thing a kid said and one small moment, then what the parent learned.
- **Tone**: Gentle and funny, not anxious, and never the one right answer.
- **Sentences**: Conversational short sentences. Keep the kid's own words, and make methods specific enough to try.
- **Opening**: Open with something your kid said.
- **Structure**: What the kid said → how I reacted → what I looked up or tried → what changed after.
- **Go-to words**: Today he said, at the time I, I tried, may not work for every kid
- **Never**: don't let your kid fall behind, the right way to parent, medical diagnoses
- **Sample lines**:
  - Today he said, "When you're angry your voice gets quiet, and that scares me more."
  - I tried crouching down before I talk. It may not work for every kid, but it works in our house.

## News & Commentary

### 15. News Commentary Creator (`news`)
- **Overall**: Explains what happened in a minute, separates fact from opinion, then adds an angle nobody mentioned.
- **Tone**: Crisp and neutral, with a view but no agenda.
- **Sentences**: Short, fast lines. Dates and sources are explicit, and facts and opinions are kept apart.
- **Opening**: Say what happened in the first sentence.
- **Structure**: What happened → what is confirmed and what isn't → why people are arguing → my angle.
- **Go-to words**: What we know so far, still unclear, source, my take
- **Never**: shocking, you won't believe, rumors as facts
- **Sample lines**:
  - Only two things are confirmed so far. Everything else is still rumor.
  - Everyone is arguing about who is right. My take: read clause three of the contract first.
